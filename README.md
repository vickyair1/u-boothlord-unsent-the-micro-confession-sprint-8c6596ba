## The entry

> To: my ex's mother
> I still make your soup when I'm sick, and your son still thinks I learned it from a cookbook.

112 characters. The addressee and the message need each other: the soup is hers, the son is hers, and the secret — that the recipe survived the breakup and he never knew where it came from — only makes sense sent to her. It stayed unsent because saying it would admit the bond with his mother outlived the relationship, and because telling the son would mean confessing years of quiet theft. Specific and honest (her soup, sick days, a cookbook lie), with no joke, no greeting, no explanation after it.
